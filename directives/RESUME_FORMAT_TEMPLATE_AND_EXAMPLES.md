## 2. Universal Formatting Specifications

| Attribute | Specification | Strict Constraint |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Typeface** | **Arial only** | Do not use Times New Roman, Calibri, Roboto, or serif fonts. |
| **Color Palette** | **Black and White** | Links (Email, Phone, LinkedIn) may be standard blue. No colored headings or shapes. |
| **Layout Columns** | **Single Column Only** | 0 multi-column tables, 0 sidebars, 0 text boxes, 0 graphics/icons, 0 headshots. |
| **Spacing** | **1.15 header, 1.5 body** | Single empty line between sections and jobs. 0 empty lines between title and bullets. |
| **Font Sizing** | **14pt** (Name), **12pt** (Contact/Location), **10.5pt** (All section headers and body text). |
| **Page Length** | **Can be > 1 page** | Do not cram or shrink font to fit 1 page. 2 to 3 pages is completely acceptable up to 12 years experience. |
| **Bullet Formula** | **Past Tense Always** | Bullet 1 = Summary (8-year-old explanation). Bullets 2–8 = What (keyword) + How (action) + Result/Reason. |
| **Density Target** | **75% in Top Half** | Place 75% of target qualifications in the top half of page 1. |
